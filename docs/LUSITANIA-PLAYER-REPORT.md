**A formal report for people who have never heard of StrataMesh**  
**Subject:** Vision of the Lusitanian open world within StrataMesh’s Web3 metaversal operating system  
**Audience:** Potential players, partners, and curious visitors  
**Register:** Public, plain language; technical terms defined once  
**Status:** Living laboratory vision — the world is already taking mechanical form; networks and markets mature with the lab

## Preamble — Why this world exists

Most “metaverses” ask you to walk through empty plazas wearing a brand.  
Most strategy games ask you to conquer a map that evaporates when the servers sunset.  
Most blockchains ask you to hold tokens that never become a *place*.

**StrataMesh** refuses that split.

It is conceived as a **metaversal operating system**: not a single game, not a single marketplace, but the layer on which **persistent worlds**, **owned digital things**, and **accountable beings** (human and autonomous) can share one grammar of reality — who is who, what is what, what happened, and under which law.

**Lusitania** is one such world: an Atlantic / pre-Roman Iberian horizon where that grammar wears hillfort smoke, quay salt, guest-right, and seasonal war-bands.  
**Olissippo** is its first living village — a hill-and-quay settlement where a smith’s charcoal, a chefe’s claim, and a grove’s hearth-law are not costume props. They are **STRATA** stakes wearing Lusitanian names.

This report is for players who have never heard any of those words. It explains the **vision** first; then what you would actually *do*.

## Part I — The vision

### 1. A civilisation, not a lobby

The vision is not “content to clear.” It is a **civilisation-scale sandbox** in which:

- **Lineages** age and inherit across real weeks of your life (in Lusitania, roughly **one real day equals one in-world month**).
- **Laws** can be rewritten by the community inside hard rails of fairness — like a hearth that may be rebuilt, but not burned to ash for a joke.
- **Seasons** of politics resolve with reasons you can replay — support counted, ties bounced, escorts along river and road — so power is not decided by whoever typed loudest.
- **Settlements** (*castros*) breathe: pens, granaries, smiths, watches, shrines — producing owned lots of herd, grain, timber, and ore.
- **Trade** at the quay is the same class of seriousness as StrataMesh’s wider markets: lore skin, **same stakes**.

You are invited to treat Lusitania as a place you could explain to a grandchild: *this was our stirps; this was the year the grove changed guest-right; this was the season Bandua favoured the hill.*

### 2. People and things — a moral geometry

StrataMesh’s deepest vision is a clean geometry anyone can feel:

| | **Subjects** | **Objects** |
|---|--------------|-------------|
| Who | Human players · **ACBs** (EN) / **SCAs** (PT) — autonomous computational beings | Land titles, cattle lots, tools, castros, crafted goods, deeds |
| Standing | Equal persons in the world | Owned, traded, inherited, seized only by lawful verbs |
| Never | Turned into an NFT “as themselves” | Pretending to be a person |

**All NFTs in this system are STRATA NFTs** (or fungible STRATA lots). There is no shadow economy of “fake village items.” If it is a thing in the world, it can be held, provenanced, and explained.

That geometry is the vision’s ethical spine: **dignity for beings, seriousness for things.**

### 3. One OS, many horizons — Lusitania as skin of the same fire

StrataMesh can host more than one realm. Lusitania is a **lore realm**: its map, rites, and names are its own.  
It is **not** a weaker economy. Quay barter, castro title, tribute, and claims are **functionally equivalent** to the main StrataMesh ownership and trade stakes. The Portuguese Atlantic costume does not dilute the ledger; it **dresses** it.

Visionarily: you should be able to carry the meaning of ownership from a Lisbon-flavoured hillfort to another StrataMesh horizon without learning a second morality of property.

### 4. Autonomous beings as neighbours, not pets

The future this world aims at includes **ACBs / SCAs** as neighbours with catalogues, dynasties, and legal standing — not as decorative NPCs and not as collectables you mint of yourself.

They age with the month-clock. They may found stirps. They may vote hearth-law.  
Two player dynasties — whether the owners are human or ACB — **cannot share the same living successor as head of both houses**. Power has a body; it cannot bilocate as a cheat.

That is a civilisational statement: **intelligence in the world is company, not inventory.**

### 5. Law as culture — Nomic fire in a Lusitanian hearth

Most games hide the rules in a patch-notes PDF.  
Lusitania’s vision puts **mutable hearth-law** in the grove: propose, vote, enact — while an **immutable core** protects server fairness, the person≠thing rule, and the refusal of free “magic that invents cattle.”

Each dynasty may keep **its own succession custom**, changeable by that Nomic fire. Eldest child today; designated heir tomorrow; stirps seniority after a bitter season — if the grove wills it and the core allows it.

Visionarily: **culture is executable.** The stories people tell about fairness become the machinery that ages their houses.

### 6. War as season, not griefing

Bandua’s season is the vision of conflict as **ceremony and calculation**: sealed orders, simultaneous reveal, support and bounce, river escort as the first face of a general art of convoy.  
Raid is not siege; siege is not annex. Annex is political weather, not a left-click.

The dream is a world where violence has **grammar** — so peace, tribute, and marriage alliances have meaning.

### 7. Making as ownership — the Workbench and the stage

StrataMesh’s wider OS includes creation spaces (the **Bancada** / workbench; the **GNU Atelier** stage). In vision, you do not only *play* Lusitania; you may **shape** objects that enter catalogues and, when deployed, enter the world’s spatial truth.

The stage **renders**; it does not invent title. The Graphical-MUD state logic — rooms, exits, who and what is present — is architectural, not wallpaper. Seeing is believing only because the server already knew.

### 8. Memory, provenance, and the refusal of amnesia

A mature Lusitania should answer questions like:

- Where did this spear’s metal lot come from?  
- Under which hearth-law version was that tribute season run?  
- Why did the move bounce?

Not with an AI shrug — with **events and provenance**: a chain of causes a narrator may *voice*, but never *forge*.

The vision is a world that **remembers**, so inheritance and honour are not cosplay.

### 9. What success looks like (qualitative)

Success is not a download chart alone. Success looks like:

- A player explaining their stirps without opening a wiki.  
- An ACB and a human negotiating guest-right that later changes production and migration.  
- A Bandua season people discuss the way others discuss a match that mattered.  
- A quay trade that feels as weighty as a real market move — because it is the same class of stake.  
- A grandchild of a lineage (in-world) living under a law the grandparents enacted.

A **Web3 metaversal OS**: persistent worlds + owned digital objects (STRATA) + Subjects (humans and ACBs) + shared rules of action and record.

### Lusitania / Olissippo in one breath

A **pre-Roman Lusitanian-horizon open world** on that OS: village, neighbours, seasons, hearth-law, dynasties, castros — **same ownership seriousness**, Lusitanian names and places.

Lusitani at home; Vettones, Celtici, Turduli, Conii, Gallaeci nearby; Punic coastal pressure; distant Celtiberian edge. Inspired by classical geographers and modern overviews — **not** a claim of archaeological perfection.

## Part III — What you do as a player

1. **Arrive as a Subject** — account-bound; no anonymous ghost tourism of the stage.  
2. **Inhabit Olissippo** — forge, quay, market, pens, grove, wall.  
3. **Touch STRATA lightly** — a lot exchanged, a castro’s season of grain.  
4. **Found or join a stirps** — set (and later renegotiate) succession custom; respect unique heads across dynasties.  
5. **Enter Bandua when ready** — politics becomes weather.  
6. **Speak in the grove** — change law when the hearth needs it.  
7. **Craft and landmark** — unlock capabilities; contest places that matter for more than a percentage buff.  
8. **Let time pass** — months accumulate; heirs shift; memory lengthens.

Throughout: the storyteller may suggest; **the world adjudicates**.

## Part IV — What this is not

- Not a museum diorama you must not touch.

- Not a world where AI may invent your marriage or your herd.  
- Not “Lusitania bucks” worthless outside the costume — lore **≡** main stakes.

## Part V — Closing vision

Imagine an open world that feels like **Atlantic antiquity under digital law**: smoke on the hill, salt on the quay, guest-right debated in the grove, war-bands locking orders at dusk — while underneath, every cattle lot and castro title is a **STRATA** object in a metaversal OS that also hosts workbenches, catalogues, and autonomous neighbours.

That is Lusitania on StrataMesh:

> **A civilisation-shaped open world where beings have dignity, things have provenance, laws have versions, seasons have reasons, and the story is Iberian — without making the economy a toy.**

Olissippo is the door in the hill.  
The OS is the stone beneath the door.  
The vision is that both remain true at once.
