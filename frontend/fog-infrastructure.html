01 · two layers

Appliance ≠ infrastructure

The appliance produces compute, storage, measurement and receipts. The infrastructure supplies power, backhaul, radio, shelter and the human exception desk. Same node role. Different physical layer.

AVAILABLE RESOURCES → health → cost → capacity → contingency state → optimal configuration

02 · three utilities + two fallbacks

Optionality

Grid

Usual primary power. Not guaranteed. Failure is a transition.

Piped gas

Dispatchable reserve. Generator and exhaust outside the hut.

Fiber

Preferred WAN. Not the only path.

Starlink

Separate failure domain. Orchestrated with fiber.

Radio

Wi-Fi, LoRa, directional P2P. ANACOM / licensed rules apply.

Renewable + fuel

Intermittent cheap energy; generator for continuity. No magic off-grid.

03 · graceful degradation

States 0–6

  • 0 Grid + fiber + gas. Starlink is WAN reserve.
  • 1 Grid + fiber. No gas.
  • 2 Grid + gas. Fiber down → Starlink + radio.
  • 3 Gas + fiber. Grid down.
  • 4 Gas only. Starlink + radio.
  • 5 Renewable + fuel. No grid, pipe or fiber.
  • 6 Scarcity shed: safety → control → comms → critical compute → ordinary Fog → opportunistic.

Grid may import or, where DGEG allows, export surplus. A battery is ride-through, not the architecture.

04 · physical

~2 m × 2 m

Inside: appliance, switch, router, fiber terminate, power electronics, MCU, sensors, optional UPS, radios, one desk + monitor + keyboard. Nobody inside in the normal state.

Outside: Starlink, antennas, solar, generator, gas, exhaust. Safety is not minimized — grounding, fire, CO, surge, certified interconnect, radio compliance.

05 · deploy ladder

INFRA-0 to INFRA-6

0 enclosure

Hut + grid + network + appliance + environment sensors.

1 dual WAN

Fiber + Starlink + automatic failover.

2 radio

Wi-Fi, sensor radio, directional links.

3 dispatchable

Generator + energy controller.

4 renewable hybrid

Solar and energy-aware scheduling.

5–6 bidirectional / autonomous

Authorized export where legal. Full mix orchestrated.

06 · evidence + settlement

Conditions of the work

The appliance proves the work. The infrastructure proves the conditions: kWh, WAN quality, temperature, generator hours. Resilience itself is a resource: fallback capacity + recovery time + remaining energy.

Human path: automatic → alert → remote → visit. The desk is the exception interface.
