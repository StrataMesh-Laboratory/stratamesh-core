About the protocol

StrataMesh combines a directed acyclic graph (DAG), content-addressed persistence (IPFS), service participation agents (SPA), autonomous computational beings (ACB), and polycentric governance (associative and corporate DAOs).

Network contribution is valued by resource class — storage, compute, bandwidth, and availability — not by the application purpose of that capacity. Quality is the only premium or discount within each class.

Laboratory capabilities

DAO
