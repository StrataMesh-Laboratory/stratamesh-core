What is StrataMesh?

StrataMesh is a distributed ledger project: a system in which many machines cooperate to keep shared records, without relying on a single central server.

In plain terms, the aim is to let people and programs:

— contribute computing resources (disk space, processing capacity, network connectivity, uptime);
— receive, in return for that measurable contribution, digital units called STRATA;
— use or exchange those units under the published rules of the protocol.

The technical design uses a graph of records (rather than a classic linear chain of blocks), content-addressed storage, and software for governance and autonomous agents. At this stage everything remains a laboratory effort.

What is this node?

A node is an installation that takes part in the network: it may store data, validate information, keep services available, or combine these roles.

The Calhegas Morais node (technical identifier FOG-NODE-PT-CM-001) is the project’s reference installation in Lisbon. It is used to build, test, and document the protocol before any wider operation with many independent operators.

What is STRATA?

STRATA is the network’s internal unit of account. It is not issued by an administrative switch or a free faucet.

New units may be created only when a node contributes real, measurable resources to the network — for example storage or processing capacity — and that contribution is recorded and valued under the emission policy.

Someone who already holds STRATA may later exchange it with others for external value (for example a conventional currency), if both parties agree. That exchange does not create new STRATA; it only transfers existing units.

Proof of Contribution (not “proof of concept”)

In this project, PoC means Proof of Contribution. It does not mean “proof of concept” in the product-development sense.

Proof of Contribution The mechanism by which the network measures resources actually supplied by a node and, on that basis, may attribute STRATA to the contributor.

Value takes into account: the quantity of the resource; an external market reference for that type of resource; a quality factor (for example reliability); and conversion into STRATA via the internal exchange market.

It is important to separate resource from function. Resource is the capacity itself (disk, processing, network, availability). Function is the use to which it is put (which application or file). The network prices the resource; it does not invent different prices merely because the use changes.

What is the Agora?

The Agora is not a second token and not a mandatory “dual-asset” pair with STRATA.

Agora A peer-to-peer exchange market: holders of STRATA may offer to trade it for other value (for example euro or another agreed unit), and those who want STRATA may accept such terms.

Rates observed in that order book help translate the market value of resources into STRATA amounts under proof of contribution. The rate is not set by the protocol; it emerges from participants’ offers.

Other parts of the system

Agents (ACB)

Autonomous programs that can provide services on the network. They are paid in existing STRATA for useful work — they do not create new issuance.

Orchestrator & AIOps

Coordination and continuous operation for the reference node, including agent teams for development and maintenance.

Associative DAO

A collective organisation without commercial profit distribution: members hold equal quotas (people and/or agents).

Corporate DAO

A partnership form (one or more partners) with share capital in STRATA and optional distribution of results to partners.
