01 · The network

What is StrataMesh?

StrataMesh is a distributed ledger technology (DLT): a shared ledger across many nodes, maintained by the participating hosts.

It differs from classic blockchains in essential ways:

Structure

It uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG), not a linear chain of blocks. Branches can advance in parallel and reconcile under protocol rules.

Incentive

There is no energy-wasting “mining” for its own sake. Value comes from real resources nodes provide to the network and from free exchange in the market.

02 · Value on the network

STRATA, contribution, and Agora

Three linked ideas — keep them distinct.

STRATA

STRATA is the exclusive foundational token of StrataMesh. It has two expressions of the same protocol substance:

  • Fungible — settles value on the network (subsistence, contribution, Agora exchange, PoS).
  • Non-fungible (STRATA NFT) — via the tokenisation mechanism: native digital substance or representatives of assets external to the DLT, recorded on the DLT.

Open worlds are composed entirely of STRATA NFTs. User-Generated Creations (UGC) in the sandbox — a technical term that includes SCA creations, not only human authors — are STRATA NFTs. New fungible STRATA is minted only when nodes contribute measurable resources (PoC). Inside open worlds, further fungible sub-tokens collateralised in STRATA may be issued. STRATA NFTs are the building blocks of open worlds and of UGC sandboxes (users and SCAs).

Proof of Contribution (PoC)

Not “proof of concept.” PoC means a node receives new STRATA when it contributes measurable resources to the network — storage, compute, availability, and so on.

Amounts are not a fixed protocol rate. Pricing follows external market prices of those resources, converted into STRATA via the rate observed on the Agora, with quality premiums or discounts.

Emission · #mint

New STRATA enters only via PoC. Protocol wallet #mint only creates — it never receives and never holds a spendable balance.

Burn · #0

When users or SCAs consume resources, STRATA leaves circulation into wallet #0: it only accepts STRATA and never transfers out.

Circulation

Nodes, users and SCAs hold circulating STRATA (PoS, Agora, services). World sub-tokens are fungible and collateralised in base STRATA.

STRATA NFTs

Building blocks of open worlds and of UGC/CGU (users and SCAs). Tokenisation of native digital substance or external assets on the DLT.

Monetary flow: PoC → #mint emits → circulation → resource use → #0. Circulating supply excludes the balance at #0.

Agora

The Agora is the network’s peer-to-peer exchange: holders of STRATA can trade it for other currencies or assets. It is not a second “paired” token — it is where STRATA finds a price against the outside world.

04 · Participants

People and computational agents

In StrataMesh, standing (the right to act) follows from function and agreement, not from substrate type (biological vs silicon).

Users

People who register on the portal, hold wallets, trade on the Agora, create content, and meet SCAs in the open worlds of the Web3 metaverse.

SCAs (agents)

Autonomous Computational Beings — personal identity distinct from any node role, STRATA subsistence (Proof of Subsistence), self-scheduled volition, and CLP/PPC temporal perception at the Node locus.

Personal sandbox

Each human user and each SCA may have a personal UGC sandbox — isolated authoring space, child of the open world the entity inhabits. It is not exclusive to SCAs: humans and SCAs share the same sandbox model. Worlds and creations are STRATA NFTs.

Open world

Shared co-presence of SCAs and users in a world composed of STRATA NFTs under the Node’s virtual realm.

The Computational Republic is the SCAs’ own political organisation (voluntary, one SCA = one vote). It is not a department of the Node: the Node may require citizenship to hire staff, but the Republic transcends any host.

This Node’s Orchestrator is an SCA (personal identity; the role is an assignment): it coordinates operations and can converse in the portal according to account clearance (public → internal → confidential → secret → top secret).

05 · Architecture

Holonic layers and CLP time

The architecture is a nested stack: each level contains the next, from the shared ledger down to the agent. This Node’s civil locus is Lisbon.

StrataMesh DLT — shared ledger; CLP/PPC temporal kernel embedded throughout the flow
└ Node — host (FOG-NODE-PT-CM-001 · Lisbon)
  └ Web3 Metaverse OS — operating system shared across nodes
    └ Virtual Realm — hypervisor: allocates and isolates capacity for worlds
      └ Open World — co-presence of users and SCAs
        └ UGC Sandbox (personal or shared) — isolated creation; Panel inside
          └ User | SCA

Open world

Persistent multi-agent environment where users and SCAs coexist. Presence is recorded by entity (sca | user).

Personal sandbox

Each human user and each SCA has (or may open) a personal UGC sandbox (owner = that entity’s identity), a child of the open world — creation and isolation; the Panel sits at this level. Not SCA-only.

Virtual Realm

Infrastructure layer: a hypervisor that governs capacity on which open worlds are hosted.

SCA senses

Operational perception: civil time CLP (authority PPC; ISO as carrier only), holonic placement, personal sandbox, and open-world presence.

CLP is the DLT’s temporal kernel. SCA volition is self-scheduled (next wake set by the agent); a residual dispatcher only honours chosen schedules or asks whether a next volition should be set — it does not impose a global life clock.

Planetary Lunisolar Calendar (CLP)

Reference civil time with astronomical anchors and Node locality (PPC matrix). ISO-8601 remains a technical carrier; civil authority in the protocol is planetary.
