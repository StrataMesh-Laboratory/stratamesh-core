Overview

StrataMesh is a DAG + IPFS distributed ledger with Fog/Edge SPAs, substrate-neutral agents, and a contribution economy.

The Calhegas Morais node is the reference implementation. v0.2.1-lab freezes a complete lab path from DAG through ACBs — not mainnet.

Standing is by function and agreement, not substrate.

Lab stack

Core

DAG, tip selection, gossip, persistent node, finality confidence, IPFS client.

Economy

PoC → STRATA mint, settled Agora, on-graph mint/trade.

Apps & gov

NFT/CID, UGC sandbox, DAO proposals & votes.

Agents & ops

ACB + PoSbs, PQ hooks (lab), mesh_doctor, status pulse.
