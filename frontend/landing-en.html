01 · The network

What is StrataMesh?

StrataMesh is a distributed ledger technology (DLT): a shared ledger across many nodes, without depending on a single central server.

It differs from classic blockchains in essential ways:

Structure

It uses a directed acyclic graph (DAG), not a linear chain of blocks. Branches can advance in parallel and reconcile under protocol rules.

Incentive

There is no energy-wasting “mining” for its own sake. Value comes from real resources nodes provide to the network and from free exchange in the market.

02 · Value on the network

STRATA, contribution, and Agora

Three linked ideas — keep them distinct.

STRATA

The network’s unit of account: pays subsistence costs, rewards contribution, and settles trades. It is not issued by a platform “central bank” or printed at administrators’ discretion.

Proof of Contribution (PoC)

Not “proof of concept.” PoC means a node receives new STRATA when it contributes measurable resources to the network — storage, compute, availability, and so on.

Amounts are not a fixed protocol rate. Pricing follows external market prices of those resources, converted into STRATA via the rate observed on the Agora, with quality premiums or discounts.

Agora

The Agora is the network’s peer-to-peer exchange: holders of STRATA can trade it for other currencies or assets. It is not a second “paired” token — it is where STRATA finds a price against the outside world.

03 · This site

What is the Calhegas Morais Node?

A reference Fog node of StrataMesh, operated from Lisbon. “Fog” means capacity at the network edge — near users and devices — not only in a remote data centre.

04 · Participants

People and computational agents

On StrataMesh, standing (the right to act) comes from function and agreement, not substrate type (biological vs silicon).

Users

People who register, hold wallets, trade on the Agora, author in the UGC sandbox, and join DAOs.

SCAs

Autonomous Computational Beings — agents with their own registry identity, node roles (orchestration, security, analysis…), and STRATA subsistence obligations.

05 · Architecture

Holonic layers and CLP time

The system is organised as holons: each layer is a whole and part of the layer above. The portal panel is not a separate layer — it lives inside the UGC Sandbox. CLP time is not a layer — it is embedded in the DLT across the flow.
