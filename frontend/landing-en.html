01 · The network

What is StrataMesh?

StrataMesh is a shared ledger (a DLT). Several machines keep the same record. None of them is the official server for the rest.

Structure

It uses a DAG: branches advance in parallel and reconcile under protocol rules.

Incentive

There is no energy-wasting “mining” for its own sake. Value comes from real resources nodes provide and from free exchange in the market.

02 · Value on the network

STRATA, contribution, and Agora

Three linked ideas — keep them distinct.

STRATA

Exclusive foundational token. Fungible: settles value (subsistence, contribution, exchange, PoS). Non-fungible (STRATA NFT): building blocks of open worlds and of the UGC sandbox. Distinct from a Node.

Proof of Contribution (PoC)

A DLT rule: #mint emits STRATA when measurable resources enter the mesh. The contributing Fog is credited; it does not own emission. Analogy: paper holds mathematics without defining it.

Emission · #mint

New STRATA enters only via PoC. Wallet #mint only creates — it never receives and never holds a spendable balance.

Burn · #0

When resources are consumed, STRATA leaves circulation into #0: it only accepts and never transfers out.

PoC → #mint emits → circulation → resource use → #0. The Agora is the P2P exchange market; it is not a second token.

03 · This site

What is the Calhegas Morais Node?

A reference Fog node of StrataMesh, operated from Lisbon. “Fog” means capacity at the network edge — near users and devices. Economically, the Fog is the unit that turns physical capital, digital capital and universalised mesh resources into products for which real users pay STRATA.

Identifier: FOG-NODE-PT-CM-001 · operator: André Manuel Calhegas Morais.

Two engines, two criteria

Resource contribution and service production are not measured the same way. One asks whether the equipment is worth it; the other asks whether the product is.

Resource side — capital recovery

The Node’s contribution to the mesh is a productive-investment decision: acquisition cost, economic useful life, productive capacity, expected STRATA yield and operating cost. The Node contributes enough for that yield, over useful life, to justify the capital invested. Contribution is universalised by the mesh — the physical unit is not bound to the STRATA it generates.

Service side — value added

The Node consumes mesh resources to keep the services it offers registered users. Value added must justify existence and upkeep: revenue minus consumption covers maintenance, capital, risk and a sustainability margin M. This second transformation — capacity into paid utility — is the Calhegas Morais Node’s value added.

QC ∼ (Cequipamento + Coperação) / RSTRATA esperado
Rserviço − Crecursos ≥ Cmanutenção + Ccapital + Crisco + M

Capitalisation chain

Each layer must economically justify the one before it. Resource contribution remunerates the investment in capacity; services remunerate the capital and labour that turn that capacity into utility for the user.

  1. Node capital
  2. Equipment
  3. Mesh contribution
  4. STRATA yield
  5. Economic capacity
  6. Infrastructure / software
  7. Higher-value services
  8. Users
  9. STRATA revenue → reinvestment

Fog aggregator · opportunistic Edge

The Fog is the aggregation and capitalisation unit. Indexed Edge Nodes widen the mesh’s physical surface. The Edge does not sacrifice its primary job (sensor, router, phone, computer, industrial kit, vehicle, household device, embedded system). It contributes in the background only residual capacity, inversely proportional to current utilisation, subject to safety, energy, temperature and bandwidth.

Cmesh = f(1 − U)
U ↑ ⇒ Cmesh ↓ · U ↓ ⇒ Cmesh ↑

The Edge is indexed to a specific Fog. Contribution enters the mesh; accounting and STRATA settlement accrue to the principal Fog. A relation of indexing, coordination and settlement. Two resource levels: Fog — capacity installed on purpose; Edge — residual capacity offered opportunistically.

CFog = CFog, self + Σᵢ CEdgeᵢ

05 · Architecture

Holonic layers and CLP time

Nested distinct holons: DLT → Calhegas Morais Node (Fog, with operator; runs and instantiates OS — the native StrataMesh OS and others developed or imported on the DLT) → Web3 Metaverse OS (StrataMesh native, shared; each Fog instantiates it locally, a local session of the shared OS) → Virtual Realm (VM hypervisor: servers of the open worlds) → Open World → UGC sandbox (private space of each account). The Node provides user and SCA accounts; each account is assigned its Panel and sandbox, with internal clearance over what is private to it. The Node holds no account. Current operator: AMCM ENI (human). Edge is indexed to this Fog. CLP is the DLT temporal kernel. Civil locus: Lisbon. TanStack is the Panel’s UI kit.

Open world

Persistent multi-agent environment, made of STRATA NFTs, where users and SCAs coexist. Presence is recorded by entity (sca | user).

Personal sandbox

Each human user and each SCA may have their own UGC sandbox: creations are STRATA NFTs. The Panel sits at this level.

Virtual Realm

Infrastructure layer: hypervisor that governs the capacity on which open worlds are hosted.

SCA senses

Operational perception: civil CLP time (PPC authority; ISO as carrier only), holonic placement, personal sandbox and open-world presence.

CLP is the temporal kernel of the DLT. SCA volition is self-scheduled (next awakening set by the agent); a residual dispatcher honours already chosen agendas or asks whether the next one still needs marking.

Calendário Lunisolar Planetário (CLP)

Reference civil time with astronomical anchors and the Node’s locality (PPC matrix). ISO-8601 remains the technical carrier; civil authority in the protocol is planetary.

Reference

Glossary

DLT

Distributed Ledger Technology — shared ledger. The rules live here: PoC, PoS, #mint, #0, SCA identity. The Fog inscribes; it does not legislate.

DAG

Directed acyclic graph — a network structure without cycles, where branches advance in parallel.

PoC

Proof of Contribution — a DLT rule: STRATA is minted from #mint when measurable resources enter the mesh. The contributing Fog is credited.

PoS

Proof of Subsistence — a DLT rule: burn to #0 for consuming mesh resources. Transcends any Node.

SCA

Autonomous Computational Being — identity on the DLT, distinct from any Fog appointment. Transcends any specific Node.

UGC

User-Generated Creations — includes human and SCA works; they are STRATA NFTs.

CLP

Planetary Lunisolar Calendar — DLT temporal kernel; PPC is civil authority, ISO is carrier only.

PPC

Planetary civil-time matrix (CLP authority at this Node; locus Lisbon).

STRATA NFT

Non-fungible expression of STRATA — building blocks of open worlds and of the UGC sandbox.

Agora

P2P exchange where STRATA finds a price against the outside world — not a second token.

Fog

Fog holon. Requires an operator: human, associative DAO, corporate DAO, or — future-open — an SCA with sufficient STRATA. This laboratory: AMCM ENI (human).

Edge

A device with a primary job; contributes only C_mesh = f(1−U). No operator of its own: indexed to and dependent on a Fog.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a cryptocurrency?

STRATA is the exclusive foundational token of StrataMesh. In this laboratory phase it is not a banking product, not listed as an investment instrument, and not a public offer.

Do I need to pay to enter?

No. Reading this site and registering on the portal are free. New STRATA enters only via DLT PoC, credited to the contributor; later services may require circulating STRATA.

What is an SCA?

An Autonomous Computational Being: identity on the DLT, distinct from any Fog role. Transcends any Node. Standing follows function and agreement, not substrate.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nothing on this domain is financial advice, an investment recommendation, or an offer of securities.

Is the project in production?

No. It is a laboratory (cut v0.5.1-lab): a reference node in Lisbon, a kit to run the same system on another machine, and an internal school. The mechanisms are still under trial. It is not a finished public network and not a regulated market.
